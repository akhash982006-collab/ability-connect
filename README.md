Project Title

3roots India – Inclusive Employment Platform for Persons with Disabilities

Problem Statement

Persons with disabilities in India continue to face major barriers to employment despite existing legal protections and government schemes.

Major problems include:

Lack of job-ready skills.

Difficulty finding suitable jobs.

Employers not knowing how to hire PwD candidates.

Fear of high workplace accessibility costs.

Limited support for SMEs.

Weak employment infrastructure in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.

Lack of support after a candidate gets a job.

Job portals focusing only on job listings instead of actual employment outcomes.

Job matching systems relying on disability categories instead of actual essential job tasks and functional abilities.

The solution must address both:

Supply side – preparing skilled PwD candidates.

Demand side – helping businesses confidently hire and retain PwD employees.

The solution should not depend only on government mandates or legal requirements.

It should provide genuine business benefits for employers.

The platform must evaluate whether a candidate can perform the actual essential tasks of a job, with reasonable accommodation and assistive technology considered before determining compatibility.

Job Compatibility Engine

Calculate compatibility using:

Skills

Functional Ability

Essential Job Tasks

Assistive Technology

Workplace Accessibility

Reasonable Accommodation

Safety and Productivity Requirements

The system should never use logic such as:

"Candidate has locomotor disability → reject physical job."

Instead calculate whether the actual tasks can be completed safely and effectively, with reasonable accommodation and available assistive technology.

The engine should:

Identify each essential task.

Compare the task requirements with candidate skills and functional abilities.

Check whether the candidate can perform the task directly.

Check whether the task can be performed using an alternative method.

Check available assistive technology.

Check workplace accessibility.

Check reasonable accommodation options.

Identify unresolved safety, productivity or accessibility questions.

Provide a transparent explanation.

Refer uncertain cases for human or accessibility expert review.

The engine must not make disability-based automatic rejection decisions.

Compatibility Status

Display:

Directly Compatible

Candidate can perform the essential tasks without additional accommodation.

Compatible With Accommodation

Candidate can perform the essential tasks if reasonable accommodation or assistive technology is provided.

Requires Assessment

Compatibility cannot be automatically determined and needs human/accessibility expert review, employer discussion or candidate explanation.

Currently Not Compatible

One or more essential job tasks cannot currently be performed safely or reasonably even after available accommodation is considered.

This status must be based on specific task requirements and available options, not on disability labels.

Never display language such as:

"Not eligible because of disability."

Instead explain the specific task mismatch.

Example:

"The role includes lifting up to 10 kg as a mandatory task. The current workstation and available equipment do not provide a safe alternative. An accessibility expert review is recommended."

The system should always check reasonable accommodation before marking a role as incompatible.

Human Review Requirement

The AI matching system must not make the final rejection decision for disability-related compatibility.

When compatibility is uncertain:

Send the case to:

Accessibility Expert

or

Employer + Candidate Discussion

The candidate should also be allowed to explain how they perform the task.

Human review is required when:

Essential task requirements are unclear.

Accommodation feasibility is uncertain.

Safety cannot be assessed automatically.

Productivity requirements may be met through an alternative method.

The candidate proposes assistive technology not included in the system.

Workplace accessibility information is incomplete.

The employer and candidate disagree about task compatibility.

The system identifies a possible mismatch based only on incomplete functional information.

The review record should include:

Essential task in question.

Candidate’s proposed method.

Accommodation options considered.

Employer response.

Accessibility expert recommendation.

Final task-specific decision.

Reason for the decision.

The final decision must never be recorded as:

"Rejected because of disability."

It must describe the specific task, accommodation and evidence considered.

Hiring Decision Safeguards

The platform must:

Never automatically reject candidates based on disability category.

Never infer inability from a diagnosis or medical condition.

Never treat functional ability fields as exclusion filters.

Never rank candidates lower solely because they require accommodation.

Always check reasonable accommodation before marking a role as incompatible.

Provide transparent task-level explanations.

Allow candidate correction of inaccurate functional information.

Allow employers to revise unclear or unnecessarily restrictive job requirements.

Log compatibility decisions for audit and fairness review.

Protect sensitive personal and health-related information.

Limit access to functional information to authorized users.

Use consent-based data collection.

Provide human review for uncertain cases.

The primary measure of success should be:

Sustainable Employment.

Not simply:

Number of Registrations.

Number of Courses Completed.

Number of Applications.

The platform must never make disability-based automatic rejection decisions. It must evaluate actual essential job tasks, consider reasonable accommodation and assistive technology, provide transparent explanations, and route uncertain cases to human or accessibility expert review.
